
# Retailers for server parts

It can be hard to find specific server parts on mainstream retailers like Amazon or Newegg.

## Online Retailers

- [CTT](https://en.ctt.de): requires creating a free account to view prices, does ship internationally
- [wiredzone](https://www.wiredzone.com)
- [CDW](https://www.cdw.com/): ships internationally
- [NetworkHardwares](https://www.networkhardwares.com/): ships internationally
- [provantage](https://www.provantage.com): doesn't ship outside the US, forwarder needed
- [ServerSupply](https://www.serversupply.com/): US$500 minimum order and B2B for international orders, not sure if freight forwarders are supported
- [smicro](https://smicro.eu): unsure about shipping
- [PCNation](https://www.pcnation.com/): delivers to US addresses only, freight forwarding required
- [reichelt](https://www.reichelt.com/sg/en)
- [Lambda Tek](https://www.lambda-tek.com/shop/)
- [mitxpc](https://mitxpc.com/)
- [BLT](https://www.shopblt.com/): should ship worldwide, someone got an EPYC 9115. Only accepts bank transfers from Singapore
- [MyElectronics 19 inch rack mounts for Raspberry Pi, NUC, Apple, Mini-ITX, HP and other mini PCs](https://www.myelectronics.nl/us/)
- [ETB Technologies](https://www.etb-tech.com)
