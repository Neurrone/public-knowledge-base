Retailers for server parts

It can be hard to find specific server parts on mainstream retailers like Amazon or Newegg.

Online Retailers

  • CTT: requires creating a free account to view prices, does ship internationally
  • wiredzone
  • CDW: ships internationally
  • NetworkHardwares: ships internationally
  • provantage: doesn't ship outside the US, forwarder needed
  • ServerSupply: US$500 minimum order and B2B for international orders, not sure if freight forwarders are supported
  • smicro: unsure about shipping
  • PCNation: delivers to US addresses only, freight forwarding required
  • reichelt
  • Lambda Tek
  • mitxpc
  • BLT: should ship worldwide, someone got an EPYC 9115. Only accepts bank transfers from Singapore
  • MyElectronics 19 inch rack mounts for Raspberry Pi, NUC, Apple, Mini-ITX, HP and other mini PCs
  • ETB Technologies
